SerialSnap Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

In short: SerialSnap has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The developer never receives any of your data. The app talks only to Apple's School and Business Manager API, using the API account your organization sets up, and keeps everything else on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Connection details: your organization's name, type, Client ID and Key ID.
  • API private key: stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only, never synced to iCloud, and unlocked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • Work records: the queue of pending changes, a history (audit log) of changes sent from this device, and recently searched serial numbers.
  • Settings: app preferences, an optional admin PIN (stored only as a salted hash in the Keychain) and the access level chosen for this device.

You can export the history yourself as a CSV file. Nothing is backed up or sent anywhere by SerialSnap.

What goes to Apple

To look up and change devices, SerialSnap sends requests directly from your device to Apple (account.apple.com, api-school.apple.com and api-business.apple.com). Requests are signed with your organization's private key and include the serial numbers you look up and the device management services you assign. The key itself is never sent. Apple handles this data under your organization's agreement with Apple and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera, photos and Face ID

  • Camera: used to read serial numbers, barcodes and setup QR codes. Images are processed on the device and aren't saved or uploaded.
  • Photos: if you choose a photo to read a serial from, only that photo is read, on the device, and it isn't kept.
  • Face ID and Touch ID: handled entirely by iOS. SerialSnap only learns whether authentication succeeded.

Managed devices

If your organization's device management service sends SerialSnap a managed app configuration, the app reads the organization name, type, Client ID and Key ID from it to save you typing them.

Admin setup page

The setup page runs entirely in your browser. It makes no network requests, and the key and details you enter aren't saved or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Remove an organization in the app to delete its connection details and private key, or clear the history and recent searches in Settings. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

SerialSnap is a tool for organization administrators and technicians and isn't directed at children.
